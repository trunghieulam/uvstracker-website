Know your sun, before it burns.

UV Tracker shows the UV index where you actually are, refines it by reading the sky through your camera, and turns that into one number you can act on: how long you can safely stay out.

Free forever · no subscriptions

What it does

The UV index where you stand

Your location is used to fetch a live forecast, including the clear-sky value the sky scan needs. No account required.

A sky check with your camera

Point at open sky and the app estimates cloud cover, then reduces the clear-sky index accordingly — a local reading rather than a regional forecast.

Your skin, not an average one

A photo of your inner forearm estimates your skin tone, so the safe-sun time you see is calculated for you rather than for a notional average person.

Safe sun time

One number: how many minutes of unprotected sun stay below the sunburn threshold for your skin, at the UV level measured right now.

Free, forever

UV Tracker is free and will stay free. There is no paid tier, no subscription, and no feature held back behind a purchase. Sun safety is not something to paywall.

The app is supported by modest advertising and clearly-disclosed sun-protection product suggestions. Nothing that interrupts a reading, and never between you and a safety warning.

What it is not

UV Tracker is not a medical device. Its readings are estimates derived from public forecast data and a phone camera, and they are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you have concerns about your skin, see a doctor or dermatologist.

Never point the camera directly at the sun. It can damage your eyes and the camera sensor.

Your privacy, briefly

Photographs of the sky and of your skin are processed on your device and deleted immediately. They are never uploaded. Your skin type and settings are stored only on your phone.
