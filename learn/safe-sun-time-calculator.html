How this is calculated

1 UV index unit equals 1.5 J/m² of erythemal dose per minute, so minutes of safe sun time = MED ÷ (UV index × 1.5), where MED is your skin type's Minimal Erythemal Dose (Type I 200 J/m² through Type VI 1000 J/m²). See the full tables article for the sources.

For the sunscreen figure, the theoretical maximum is safe sun time × SPF. In practice, most people apply well under half the thickness sunscreen is tested at, so real-world protection runs far below the label number — treat the protected figure here as an optimistic ceiling, not a target, and reapply every two hours regardless.
