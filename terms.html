3. Accuracy and limitations

The readings are estimates, and their limits are worth understanding:

  • The UV index comes from a public weather forecast for your area, not a sensor on your device.
  • The sky scan estimates cloud cover from a camera image. Camera exposure and white balance vary between devices, so it is an approximation.
  • Skin-tone estimation from a photograph is affected by lighting and camera processing. It approximates a skin type; it does not diagnose one.
  • "Safe sun time" is derived from a standard erythemal-dose formula. Individual sensitivity varies with medication, medical conditions, altitude, reflection from water, sand or snow, and other factors the app cannot observe.

Treat the output as guidance that errs toward caution, not as a guarantee against sunburn or skin damage.

5. Camera and eye safety

Never point the camera directly at the sun. Doing so can permanently damage your eyes and your device's camera sensor. Aim at open sky away from the sun.
