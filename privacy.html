At a glance. UV Tracker has no accounts and no advertising. Your approximate location is sent to a weather service to fetch a UV forecast. Photographs of the sky and of your skin are processed on your device and deleted immediately — they are never uploaded. Everything else stays on your phone.

This policy describes the UV Tracker mobile application ("the app"), published by the UV Tracker project ("we", "us"). It describes the app as it exists today. If we add accounts, cloud sync or advertising, this policy will be updated and re-versioned before those features ship, and the change will be noted in Changes to this policy.

"Collected" follows Play's definition: data transmitted off your device. Data that stays on your phone, or is processed and discarded, is not collected. The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or any account identifier — because it has no accounts. It contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no third-party tracking.

2. Location, specifically

The app requests location permission only while it is in use, and requests coarse accuracy — the UV index varies over roughly ten-kilometre areas, so a precise fix is unnecessary. Your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo, an open weather API, to retrieve the forecast. Their handling of that request is governed by Open-Meteo's terms and privacy statement. We send no identifier with the request, so it is not linked to you or your device.

If you decline location permission, the app cannot fetch a local forecast. Nothing else is affected.

3. Camera and images

The camera is used for two features: reading cloud cover from the sky, and estimating skin tone from a photograph of your inner forearm.

Sky frames are analysed as they arrive and are never saved. The forearm photograph is written to a temporary file by the operating system's camera, read into memory to measure its average colour, and then deleted straight away — including when the reading fails. Neither image is uploaded, transmitted, or retained.

What is kept is the result: an estimated skin type and a colour measurement, stored on your device only.

4. Where your data is stored

All data the app keeps is held in your device's private application storage. We operate no servers and hold no copy of it. Uninstalling the app removes it permanently.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data. The only outbound network request the app makes is the forecast lookup described in section 2.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Revoke permissions — location and camera access can be withdrawn at any time in your device settings.
  • Delete your data — clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it erases everything, immediately and permanently.
  • Access and portability — since nothing leaves your device, there is no copy for us to provide.

If you are in the EU, UK, or another region with comparable law, the lawful basis for processing is your consent, given by granting the relevant permission, and withdrawn by revoking it.

7. Children

UV Tracker is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Since the app collects no personal identifiers at all, it holds no such data to remove.

8. Not a medical device

The app's readings are estimates derived from public forecast data and a phone camera. They are not a medical device and not medical advice. Do not rely on them for diagnosis or treatment of any condition.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app's data handling changes, this page is updated and its version and effective date raised before the change reaches you. Material changes — for instance introducing accounts, cloud sync, or advertising — will also be surfaced in the app itself.
